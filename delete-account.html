How to delete your Interview Mentor account and data

Interview Mentor by GlobalNova Technologies. You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  • Open Interview Mentor and go to Profile.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.
  • Your account, backed-up interview history and purchase records are deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

  • Email ahmiikhanx885@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Interview Mentor account".
  • Send it from the Google account you signed in with, so we can confirm it is yours.
  • We delete your account and data within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email address and user ID)
  • Interview history backed up in the cloud
  • Records of Premium purchases kept by us

What is kept

  • Google Play keeps its own record of purchases under Google's policies. A Premium purchase stays linked to your Google account and can be restored if you reinstall.
  • History stored on your phone stays until you tap Profile → Reset Data or uninstall the app.
