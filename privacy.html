Who we are

Interview Mentor ("the app") is published by GlobalNova Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Microphone and speech

The app asks for microphone permission so you can answer interview questions out loud. While you answer, your speech is converted to text by your device's built-in speech recognition service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google). The app does not record, store or upload audio.

If you use the app as a guest

Your answers, scores, interview history, name (if you enter one) and settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you choose "Continue with Google", we receive and store, in Google Firebase:

  • Your Google account name, email address and a user ID
  • Your interview history: job role, number of questions answered, score and date
  • Records of Premium purchases (order ID and purchase token from Google Play)

We use this only to back up your history, show it on any phone you sign in on, and to restore and support your Premium purchase. The text of your spoken answers is not uploaded.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or bank information.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data. Data is stored with Google Firebase (our service provider) and encrypted in transit.

Deleting your data and account

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything backed up in the cloud straight away.
  • Data on your phone: Profile → Reset Data, or uninstall the app.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Children

The app is made for job seekers aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the date above if the way we handle data changes.
