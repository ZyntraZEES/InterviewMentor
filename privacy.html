Who we are

Interview Mentor ("the app") is published by GlobalNova Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Microphone and speech

The app asks for microphone permission so you can answer interview questions out loud. While you answer, your speech is converted to text by your device's built-in speech recognition service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google). The app does not record, store or upload audio. Your device's speech service may process audio under its own privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

  • Your answers as text, scores and interview history
  • Your name, if you choose to enter one
  • Your settings, such as language, dark mode and voice speed

This information never leaves your device. You can delete all of it at any time from Profile → Reset Data, or by uninstalling the app.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to hear your spoken answers.
  • Internet: used by your device's speech service and to load fonts.

Children

The app is made for job seekers aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as accounts, cloud sync, AI feedback or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before those features go live and change the date above.
